# FluxFile — operational role and handoff
Revision: October 28, 2026. Planning and acceptance criteria; not a statement that these checks have passed.

## Role
File conversion and preparation of documentation, research records and media.

This project can support Groundstate's own programs before any standalone commercial offering. Paid services and future source publication are project-specific decisions. Existing licenses and contribution rights remain controlling; this document grants no new rights or release authority.

## Demonstration to capture
Convert a supported synthetic batch, inspect outputs and exercise queue/session recovery.

## Boundaries
Document optional converter dependencies and format limitations; keep original files and private paths out of public evidence.
